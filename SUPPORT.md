# SUPPORT — OspreyDB Pro

## 100% BYOC (Bring Your Own Cloud)

OspreyDB Pro is deployed **entirely inside your own AWS, GCP, Azure, or on-premises infrastructure**.
There is no OspreyDB-managed SaaS control plane, no telemetry phone-home, and no multi-tenant gateway.
You own the S3 buckets, the EC2/GCE instances, the IAM roles, and the WAL disks.

## Perpetual License with 1 Year of Updates

Every commercial license is **perpetual** for the purchased major version series. The license fee
includes **12 months of updates** (minor and patch releases). After year one, a renewal extends
update eligibility for another year. The licensed binary **continues to run indefinitely** even
if the update subscription lapses — there is no remote kill-switch.

## Support Channels

| Channel              | Scope                                        | Response Target      |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| GitHub Issues        | Bug reports, feature requests, documentation | < 48 business hours  |
| `support@ospreydb.dev` | Licensing, billing, security disclosures  | < 48 business hours  |
| Community Discord    | Community Q&A, best-effort                   | Best effort          |

All support is **strictly asynchronous**. There is no 24/7 on-call SLA, no PagerDuty escalation,
and no guaranteed phone/video support. Enterprise customers may negotiate a dedicated Slack
channel during procurement.

## Enterprise SLA Addendum (Optional)

A **24/7 PagerDuty on-call SLA with 4-hour response** and a named Technical Account Manager (TAM)
is available as a separately priced add-on for Enterprise license holders. Contact
`enterprise@ospreydb.dev` for a custom SOW.
